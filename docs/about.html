Anna Melnykova - photographer, director, curator.

As a teacher Anna developed her own study program in the field of visual arts and taught at the Bird in Flight school (Kiyv), Pinchuck art center (Kiyv), Karazin National University (Kharkiv), Kharkiv Academy of Design and Arts.

In 2020, during the lockdown, she created the Ukrainian Women Photographers Organization which already had two projects and successful exhibitions around Europe.

Curator of international exhibitions and residencies in the USA, Switzerland, Germany and the Netherlands. As an artist she is currently researching and working with themes of psychology antropology and society through experimental techniques in photography.

Her photographs are in private collections in the Netherlands, Italy, Belgium.

Her first book “Largo” dedicated to the study of gender in modern society was represented during the UNSEEN festival in Amsterdam.
