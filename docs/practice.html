Practice extends my artistic research into guided work with artists, creatives and kids.

My practice is grounded in archetypal structures, cyclical time and the relationship between body, memory and landscape.

I work with artists and those who seek depth, clarity and structural coherence in their projects.

Rather than offering solutions, I facilitate processes — attentive, rigorous and reflective.

The Interval Method maps creative work through rupture, flow, grounding and distance.

Each project — whether individual mentoring or collective workshop — becomes a passage through these phases.

Focused work within the elemental cycle.

Creative work aligned with temporal and natural rhythms.


The method is offered through different temporal formats — individual, collective and seasonal.
